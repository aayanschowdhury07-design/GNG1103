Patient guide

Before your visit.

How do I book?

Choose “Book a visit”. It opens the ClinicFlow app, or the App Store or Google Play if you don’t have it yet. Pick a service and a time, and confirm with a code we text you. No account or password needed.

What should I bring?

Your Ontario health card, a list of any medications, and comfortable clothes you can move in. If you have a referral or imaging reports, bring those too.

Can I change or cancel my visit?

Yes, in the app, free of charge until 24 hours before your visit. Closer than that, please call us.

How do I check in?

Tap “I’m here” in the app when you arrive, or type the last four digits of your phone number on the tablet at the front desk.

Is my information private?

Your booking details are stored in Canada, and only our staff can see them. Your medical records stay with your clinician, not in the booking app.
