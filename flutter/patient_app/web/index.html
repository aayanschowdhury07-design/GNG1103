How it works

Booked in a minute, reminded before you forget

  1. Find your clinic

    Search the directory or open your clinic’s booking link. Only clinics our team has checked take bookings.

  2. Pick a time

    Choose a service, a clinician or the first one available, and a time. It’s held for you while you finish.

  3. Get reminded, then check in

    A text 2 days and 1 day before. On the day, tap “I’m here” from the parking lot.

  • Today, in orderWho’s here, who’s waiting and what needs you, on one screen.
